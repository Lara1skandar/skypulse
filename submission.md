### Submission description

SkyPulse is an interactive weather dashboard I built to make checking the weather feel more visual and engaging. Users can search for any city or use their current location, and the whole interface changes based on the live conditions — sunny weather becomes warm and golden, rain creates a cool animated storm atmosphere, snow gets a crisp light theme, and thunderstorms trigger a darker electric look. The app also includes a 24-hour temperature chart, a seven-day forecast, saved cities, sunrise/sunset and moon information, historical weather lookup, shareable city URLs, unit conversion, and a quote of the day. It is built entirely with HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript and runs as a static GitHub Pages site with no backend.
